Smash Burger Recipe

This perfeclty crafted smash burger recipe will give you the best burger youve ever tasted. From it's juciy patties to it's fresh vegtables, this burger is perfect for any occasion.

Ingrients

  • 80/20 ground beef
  • Lettuce
  • Tamato
  • Onion
  • Any suace you prefer

Steps

  1. Prepare the ingredients
    • Ground beef (80/20 preferred)
    • Salt and pepper
    • Soft burger buns
    • Slices of cheese (American or your choice)
    • Toppings (lettuce, tomato, pickles, onion, sauces)
  2. Shape the patties
    • Roll ground beef into loose balls (about 3–4 oz each)
    • Don’t pack them too tightly
  3. Heat the pan or griddle
    • Use a cast iron pan or flat griddle over medium-high heat
    • Add a little oil or butter if desired
  4. Smash the burgers
    • Place the beef ball on the hot surface
    • Immediately press it flat using a spatula or burger press until thin
    • Hold for a few seconds to form a crust
  5. Season and cook
    • Sprinkle salt and pepper on top
    • Cook for ~2 minutes until edges are crispy
  6. Flip and add cheese
    • Flip the patty carefully
    • Place cheese on top
    • Cook another 1–2 minutes
  7. Toast the buns
    • While burgers finish, lightly toast the buns on the pan or griddle
  8. Assemble the burger
    • Place patties on buns
    • Add desired toppings and sauces
  9. Serve and enjoy!
    • Best eaten immediately while hot and juicy
